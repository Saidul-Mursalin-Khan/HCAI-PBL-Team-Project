Participant information & consent

This is a course-project prototype for comparing two movie preference interfaces. There are no right or wrong answers, and you may stop at any time by closing the page.

What happens

  • You make ten two-movie choices and rank two sets of ten movies.
  • You answer short usability questions and twenty held-out validation choices.
  • The application stores pseudonymous choices, rankings and response times under a random study ID.
